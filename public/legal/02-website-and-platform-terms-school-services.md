## 3. Assessment limitations

GYS results are educational and informational. They:

- are one source of information and should be interpreted with other educational context;
- are not a medical, psychological, psychiatric, or clinical diagnosis;
- do not guarantee admission, scholarship, employment, academic success, a particular career outcome, or a school ranking;
- may change as a student completes additional assessments or as norms and comparison cohorts are updated; and
- should not be used as the sole basis for a high-impact decision about a student.

Percentiles compare performance with the applicable norm group; they are not the percentage of questions answered correctly. Adaptive assessments may present different questions and difficulty paths to different students.

GYS may update assessment content, scoring methods, security controls, and non-material platform features to maintain validity, security, and service quality. A material reduction in a school’s purchased service will be handled under the applicable order form.
