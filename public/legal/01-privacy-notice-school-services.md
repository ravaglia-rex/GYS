## 2. Scope

This Notice explains how personal data is handled when an Indian school obtains GYS services for its students and when school representatives use the GYS website or platform.

It does not govern a student or parent who purchases or subscribes directly. Direct-to-family participation will be subject to a separate privacy notice, terms, age-verification process, and parental-consent flow.

## 3. Who decides how student data is used

### When a school arranges GYS services

The participating school normally decides:

- which students participate;
- which assessments and optional modules are made available;
- when assessments take place;
- which school personnel may view results;
- whether permitted aggregate school-wide participation and performance information is supplied to EducationWorld for a school-level ranking input; and
- how long the school requires identifiable reports to be available, subject to the applicable agreement and law.

For those activities, the school is intended to act as the **Data Fiduciary** and the GYS Operator acts as the school’s **Data Processor**. We process student personal data on the school’s documented instructions to administer the selected services.

Parents, guardians, and students should ordinarily contact the school first to exercise privacy rights or ask how the school uses GYS results. We will assist the school in responding.

### When the GYS Operator decides a purpose

The GYS Operator acts as a Data Fiduciary for personal data it uses for its own limited business purposes, such as:

- managing the school’s service agreement, billing, and authorised school contacts;
- securing and administering the website and platform;
- responding to support requests sent directly to us;
- preventing fraud, misuse, and security incidents; and
- complying with applicable law and enforcing legal claims.

The current school service does not include any independently determined use of identifiable student data. If we propose such a use in the future, we will first update the applicable documents, provide a separate notice, and obtain any consent required by law.

## 4. Personal data we handle

The actual data depends on the services the school selects.

### School and authorised-user data

- school name, branch, address, affiliation, and institutional identifiers;
- names, roles, work contact details, and account details of authorised school personnel;
- service selections, instructions, support records, contract records, and billing information; and
- log-in, device, network, browser, access, audit, and security-event data.

### Student data

- a school-issued student or examination identifier;
- student name only where the school elects to provide or display it;
- grade, class, section, school, and academic year;
- an age-status flag, such as “under 18,” “18 or over,” or “not confirmed”; GYS does not need a full date of birth for the standard school service;
- account credentials or contact details only if the school-selected configuration requires them;
- assessment questions presented, answers, response times, completion status, adaptive difficulty path, and retake history;
- calculated scores, percentiles, performance tiers, category-level strengths and growth areas, and reports;
- where selected, responses and results concerning reasoning, English proficiency, AI proficiency, personality, interests, motivations, stream guidance, career exploration, and university-fit guidance;
- practice-test activity, question-of-the-day activity, and platform progress;
- technical, device, browser, IP address, session, access, diagnostic, audit, and security data;
- communications and support requests; and
- **only if expressly enabled by the school**, camera, microphone, proctoring recordings, and exam-integrity signals for an AI-proctored assessment.

The school and students should not provide Aadhaar numbers, government identity documents, full dates of birth, financial information, medical information, or other sensitive information unless GYS has expressly requested the specific information for a disclosed and lawful purpose.

## 5. Why we process the data

Except for the separately selected and consented Access USA scholarship flow described below and processing required by applicable law, we use student personal data only to provide, administer, secure, support, and report on the school-selected GYS services. This includes producing individual student analytics and reports and class, grade, cohort, and school analytics and reports. We do not use identifiable student data for an unrelated or independently determined purpose.

Acting on the school’s instructions, we use student data to:

- create and administer the school’s roster and permitted student access;
- deliver adaptive assessments, practice activities, and selected modules;
- authenticate users and maintain assessment integrity;
- calculate scores, percentiles, performance tiers, and category-level results;
- generate individual, class, grade, cohort, and school reports;
- provide stream, career, skills, and university guidance where the school has selected those modules;
- maintain an agreed longitudinal profile where the school has selected that service;
- enable authorised school personnel, and parents or students through school-approved channels, to access permitted results;
- provide school-level benchmarking and analytics;
- create and provide EducationWorld with aggregate school-wide participation and performance information only where the school agreement or instruction permits it and only after controls are applied so no individual student is identifiable;
- if the optional scholarship-matching feature is enabled and consented, compare student results with qualification criteria submitted by Access USA and notify qualifying students within the GYS platform without disclosing student data to Access USA;
- provide technical support, maintain service reliability, and secure the platform; and
- return, export, correct, restrict, or delete data on the school’s instructions.

As part of providing and improving the school-selected GYS services, we may create anonymous cohort statistics for assessment validation, benchmarking, security, service improvement, and analysis of longitudinal patterns and associations between educational programs and student outcomes. We apply anonymization and aggregation controls so that no individual student is identifiable, do not retain a student-level re-identification key in the anonymous dataset, and do not attempt to re-identify a student.

If a qualifying student, or the student’s parent/guardian where required, wants to receive scholarship information, GYS will present a second, separate opt-in. That opt-in will:

- identify Access USA as the recipient and a separate Data Fiduciary;
- itemise the exact data proposed to be shared;
- explain that the data will be used to provide information about the relevant scholarship opportunity;
- link to Access USA’s privacy notice and privacy contact; and
- require a clear affirmative choice to share the data.

Access USA will not receive student data unless that second data-sharing opt-in has been completed. It will receive only the data itemised in the opt-in.

## 6. Children’s personal data and parental consent

For purposes of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a child is an individual under 18.

In the standard school service:

- the school determines whether a participating student is under 18;
- the school provides the required notice to the parent, lawful guardian, or adult student;
- the school obtains and records valid consent, including verifiable parental consent where required;
- the school instructs us to process the student’s data for the selected services; and
- we process the data on the school’s behalf under our agreement with the school.

Because grade level does not reliably establish age, we treat a student whose age status is not confirmed as under 18 for activation and consent controls.

We do not sell student personal data. We do not use student personal data for targeted advertising. We do not use identifiable school-service student data to train a general-purpose artificial-intelligence model or to market unrelated products.

We do not track students across unrelated websites or services. Within the GYS service, we process assessment activity, response patterns, progress, and security events only to deliver the school-selected educational activities, maintain assessment integrity and security, and produce the school-selected reports, after the required notice and consent.

The school may withdraw its instruction or communicate a parent’s, guardian’s, or adult student’s withdrawal of consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully completed, but the student may no longer be able to use services that require the data.

## 7. Who receives the data

We disclose personal data only as described below and only to the extent necessary:

- **The participating school:** authorised school personnel receive the student and school reports selected by the school.
- **Parents, guardians, and adult students:** reports may be provided through the school or a school-approved access method.
- **GYS service providers and subprocessors:** hosting, security, communications, assessment delivery, analytics, support, and related providers process data under written terms that require appropriate confidentiality and security. The current list is available in [Schedule 3 of the School Data Processing Terms](/in/data-processing/schools#schedule-3).
- **EducationWorld:** receives only aggregate school-wide participation and performance information prepared so no individual student is identifiable. EducationWorld does not receive student names, identifiers, responses, profiles, scores, reports, contact details, or other individual student data.
- **Access USA:** receives no student data when it submits qualification criteria or when Argus applies those criteria. Argus notifies qualifying students within GYS. Access USA receives only the itemised data covered by a qualifying student’s or parent/guardian’s second, specific data-sharing opt-in, after which Access USA acts as a separate Data Fiduciary for that data.
- **Professional advisers and transaction participants:** where reasonably necessary and subject to confidentiality protections.
- **Authorities or other persons required by law:** when disclosure is legally required or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not disclose student personal data to reward merchants, scholarship providers, universities, or marketing partners under the core school-processor service. A later disclosure to Access USA occurs only after the separate scholarship data-sharing notice and affirmative opt-in described above. Any other disclosure following a voluntary opt-in must likewise be covered by a separate notice, appropriate terms, and the consent required for that disclosure.

## 9. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for the period required for the disclosed purpose, the school’s documented instructions, and applicable law.

The retention periods below are maximum periods, not minimum periods, except where applicable law requires a minimum. We may delete data sooner when the school instructs us to do so, consent is validly withdrawn, the applicable purpose ends, or the data is no longer needed. A narrowly scoped legal hold or other legal requirement may require longer retention.

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Current roster details, such as name, contact details, class, and section, that are not needed for continuing account access or an enabled longitudinal profile | Until 90 days after the end of the applicable academic year |
| Minimal account information and the mapping between a student and a randomly generated GYS longitudinal identifier, where a longitudinal profile is enabled | Until 12 months after the school-recorded completion of Grade 12; normally no more than eight years after the first assessment, or up to nine years only if the school records a revised completion year |
| Raw assessment answers, item-level responses, response times, adaptive paths, and detailed assessment-event data | Until 24 months after the applicable result becomes final |
| Scores and reports where a longitudinal profile is not enabled | For the school service term, followed by the return and deletion periods in the Data Processing Addendum |
| Scores, reports, and derived longitudinal profiles where the feature is enabled | Until 12 months after the school-recorded completion of Grade 12; normally no more than eight years after the first assessment, or up to nine years only if the school records a revised completion year |
| AI-proctoring recordings, if enabled | Until 30 days after the result becomes final; if an integrity review or appeal is opened, until 30 days after it closes; in all cases no more than 180 days after the assessment unless a legal hold applies |
| Proctoring flags and appeal records, if enabled | Rolling 12 months after the flag is resolved or the appeal closes |
| Support communications | Rolling 12 months after the support matter closes |
| Consent, notice-version, withdrawal, and deletion evidence | Three years after the related profile is deleted, consent is withdrawn, or the applicable school relationship ends; limited to the evidence needed to demonstrate the action |
| Security, access, and processing logs | Rolling 12 months from the event; longer only for a documented incident, investigation, legal hold, or other legal requirement |
| Contract, invoice, tax, and legal records | Eight years after the relevant transaction or end of the relationship, or longer if applicable law requires; these records are not intended to contain student assessment data |
| Backups after deletion from active systems | Overwritten or deleted within 90 days after production deletion and not restored to ordinary use |

These periods apply only while the school continues to instruct GYS to provide the applicable service. A school that wants Grade 6–12 continuity must keep the longitudinal feature active through its GYS agreement or a documented longitudinal archive arrangement. When the entire school relationship, including any such arrangement, ends, we provide the school’s export period and delete student data on the earlier timetable stated in the Data Processing Addendum. We do not retain a former school’s student data merely because it might be useful in the future.

For a longitudinal profile, we separate direct identity information from the assessment record where reasonably practicable. The longitudinal record is designed to use a randomly generated identifier and retain only the grade and academic year, school and curriculum information, assessment dates, scores, report content, and derived progress measures needed for the selected service. Raw item responses, detailed assessment events, device data, and support records do not remain in the longitudinal profile after their shorter periods expire.

Information is pseudonymous, not anonymous, if Argus, the school, or another person using reasonably available information can connect an identifier to a student. Pseudonymous information remains personal data and is subject to the periods above.

GYS may create irreversibly anonymized and appropriately aggregated statistics that no longer identify an individual student for assessment validation, service benchmarking, security, improvement of the GYS services, and analysis of longitudinal patterns and associations between educational programs and student outcomes. Such anonymous statistics may be retained without a fixed period. GYS does not use them for unrelated products or third-party marketing, does not retain a student-level re-identification key in the anonymous dataset, and does not attempt to re-identify a student.

Any data retained because applicable law requires it is isolated from ordinary use, used only for the legal-retention purpose, and deleted when that requirement ends.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of student data. The controls include, as applicable:

- encryption in transit and at rest;
- role-based access and multi-factor authentication for privileged or school-administrator access;
- separation of school accounts and data;
- access, audit, and security logging;
- vulnerability management, secure development, and change controls;
- backup, recovery, and business-continuity measures;
- confidentiality and security obligations for personnel and subprocessors; and
- incident response, investigation, remediation, and notification procedures.

No system is completely secure. The school and users must protect their credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.

## 11. Personal-data breaches

If we become aware of a personal-data breach affecting data processed for a school, we will notify the school without undue delay in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum, provide available information, and support the school’s response. The school, as Data Fiduciary, is responsible for notifications to affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board of India, unless applicable law requires us to notify directly.

## 12. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, a Data Principal may have rights to:

- obtain information about personal-data processing;
- correct, complete, or update personal data;
- request erasure;
- withdraw consent as easily as it was given;
- use the applicable grievance-redressal process; and
- nominate another individual to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity.

For student data controlled by a school, contact the school using the privacy details in the school’s notice. We will assist the school with verified requests.

For data for which the GYS Operator is the Data Fiduciary, contact **globalyoungscholar@argus.ai**. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify the requester and locate the relevant records. Our grievance-response period is **60 days**.

If a grievance is not resolved through the applicable Data Fiduciary’s process, the Data Principal may use the complaint process made available by the Data Protection Board of India when the relevant provisions are in force.

## 13. Cookies and similar technologies

The GYS website and platform use cookies, local browser storage, and similar technologies as described below. We do not use advertising pixels or cross-site advertising trackers on the school-service flows. There is currently no separate cookie-consent banner because the technologies in use are limited to those needed to operate the service, plus optional product analytics and error monitoring when configured.

### Strictly necessary / functional

| Technology | Provider / storage | Purpose | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firebase Authentication session | Google Firebase (browser IndexedDB / Auth persistence) | Keep the user signed in, protect authenticated routes, and maintain session security | Until sign-out or token refresh / expiry |
| Application state persistence | First-party `localStorage` (redux-persist) | Remember signed-in session context needed for the dashboard experience | Until cleared by the user or sign-out flows |
| School / student registration drafts | First-party `localStorage` | Preserve in-progress registration form fields if the browser is refreshed | Until the draft is completed or cleared |
| Practice-mode and UI preferences | First-party `localStorage` / `sessionStorage` | Remember practice progress, tutorial dismissals, and similar non-marketing preferences | Session or until cleared |
| Payment checkout script | Razorpay (loaded only on payment pages) | Process the requested payment | Session / as set by Razorpay for checkout |

### Analytics and diagnostics (when enabled)

| Technology | Provider | Purpose | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product analytics | Segment (`@segment/analytics-next`), when a write key is configured | Understand product usage (for example sign-up and identify events). Destinations connected in Segment may set their own cookies. | Per Segment / destination defaults |
| Error monitoring | Sentry browser SDK | Detect and diagnose application errors and performance issues. Session Replay is disabled. | Per Sentry defaults |

A Google Analytics measurement ID may appear in environment configuration, but the Google Analytics SDK is not initialised in the current application code and is not relied on for school-service analytics.

We do not sell personal data collected through these technologies and do not use them to track students across unrelated websites. If we introduce additional optional analytics, advertising, or non-essential cookies, we will update this section and provide any choice required by applicable law.
