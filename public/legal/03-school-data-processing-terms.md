# Global Young Scholar School Data Processing Terms - India

**Version/effective date:** 1 July 2026

These School Data Processing Terms (“**Data Terms**”) are incorporated into the Global Young Scholar School Terms of Service and apply to every school registration and order under which **Argus Futures, Inc.**, 2261 Market Street, Suite 4230, San Francisco, California 94114, USA (“**Provider**”), processes Personal Data on behalf of a participating school.

The “**School**” is the institution identified in the applicable registration, checkout, order confirmation, or separately negotiated order. By accepting the School Terms of Service during registration or checkout, an authorised representative accepts these Data Terms on behalf of the School.

The School Terms of Service, these Data Terms, the applicable registration or order confirmation, and any separately negotiated order are together the “**Agreement**.” These Data Terms take effect when the School accepts the School Terms of Service and apply before the School submits, or permits Provider to process, any student Personal Data.

The current version of these Data Terms is linked during school registration and checkout and is available through the School dashboard. Provider may be contacted at **globalyoungscholar@argus.ai**.

## 1. Definitions

“**Applicable Data Protection Law**” means the laws and binding rules applicable to the processing under these Data Terms, including, as and when in force, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“**DPDP Act**”) and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (“**DPDP Rules**”), and during the applicable transition the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

“**Child**,” “**Consent**,” “**Data Fiduciary**,” “**Data Principal**,” “**Data Processor**,” “**Personal Data**,” “**Personal Data Breach**,” and “**Processing**” have the meanings given by Applicable Data Protection Law.

“**School Personal Data**” means Personal Data processed by Provider on School’s behalf under the Agreement.

“**Subprocessor**” means a third party engaged by Provider to process School Personal Data on School’s behalf.

## 2. Roles and scope

### 2.1 School-led processing

For School Personal Data:

- School determines the participating students, assessment purposes, selected modules, timing, permitted users, report recipients, and optional uses;
- School is the Data Fiduciary; and
- Provider is the Data Processor acting on School’s documented instructions.

School remains responsible for its obligations as Data Fiduciary, including the validity of its instructions, notices, consent, accuracy where data is used for a decision, rights responses, retention decisions, and notifications to Data Principals and the Data Protection Board of India.

### 2.2 Provider-controlled processing

Provider is a separate Data Fiduciary for business-contact, contract, invoice, legal, security, and support data that Provider processes for its own administration and legal compliance as described in the Privacy Notice.

Provider does not use identifiable student data for an independently determined purpose under the current school-service model. Provider will not treat identifiable student data as Provider-controlled data merely by describing an activity as service improvement. No independently determined use is authorised by these Data Terms. Before introducing such a use, the parties must amend the applicable documents and complete a documented role assessment, an appropriate separate notice, and a valid legal-ground and consent analysis, including parental consent where required.

### 2.3 No change by label

The parties will update these Data Terms if their actual decisions or processing activities change the role allocation. A contractual label does not override the parties’ actual conduct under Applicable Data Protection Law.

## 3. School instructions

Provider will process School Personal Data only:

1. to provide the modules and features identified in the registration, order confirmation, account configuration, or separately negotiated order, including individual student analytics and reports and class, grade, cohort, and school analytics and reports;
2. as configured by an authorised School administrator;
3. as necessary to secure, support, maintain, and troubleshoot those Services;
4. on another documented instruction from School that is within the disclosed purpose of providing the Services; or
5. as required by applicable law, in which case Provider will notify School before processing unless the law prohibits notice.

Provider will promptly notify School if Provider reasonably believes an instruction violates Applicable Data Protection Law and may pause the affected processing while the parties resolve the issue.

Except for the separately selected and consented Access USA scholarship flow described below and processing required by applicable law, Provider will use School Personal Data only to provide, administer, secure, support, maintain, and report on the School-selected Services. Provider will not use School Personal Data for an unrelated purpose.

Provider will not:

- sell School Personal Data;
- use it for targeted advertising;
- track a student across unrelated websites or services;
- undertake tracking or behavioural monitoring within the Services except as necessary for the School-selected educational activity, assessment integrity, security, and reporting described in the applicable notice and covered by the required consent;
- use it to market unrelated services to a student or parent;
- provide it to a reward merchant, scholarship provider, university, or other opportunity provider unless School expressly instructs the disclosure and all required notices and consents are in place;
- combine it with personal data obtained for an independently determined purpose; or
- use identifiable School Personal Data to train a general-purpose artificial-intelligence model.

Provider may create and use irreversibly anonymized and appropriately aggregated information for assessment validation, statistical analysis, benchmarking, security, improvement of the GYS services, and analysis of longitudinal patterns and associations between educational programs and student outcomes only if no individual student is reasonably identifiable, Provider does not retain a student-level re-identification key in the anonymous dataset, Provider does not attempt re-identification, and Provider contractually prohibits a recipient from attempting re-identification. Information remains School Personal Data while Provider, School, or another person using reasonably available information can connect a pseudonymous identifier to a student.

Where School’s agreement or documented instruction permits an EducationWorld school-ranking data contribution, Provider may supply EducationWorld only aggregate school-wide participation and performance information prepared so that no individual student is identifiable. EducationWorld will not receive School Personal Data.

Access USA supplies scholarship qualification criteria but does not receive School Personal Data during the matching process. If School enables the optional scholarship-matching feature and the required consent is recorded, Provider may apply the criteria within GYS and notify qualifying students. A qualifying student, or parent/guardian where required, may then separately opt in both to receive scholarship information and to have the itemised data shown in that notice shared with Access USA. Provider will not disclose School Personal Data to Access USA unless this second, specific data-sharing opt-in has been completed. Access USA becomes a separate Data Fiduciary for the data it receives and must provide its own privacy notice and rights contact.

## 4. School obligations

School warrants and will ensure that:

1. its instructions are lawful and within its authority;
2. it provides each Data Principal with a clear notice that itemises the data and purposes before requesting consent;
3. it offers the notice in English and any other language required for the relevant student or parent;
4. it determines from reliable records whether each participating student is under 18;
5. it obtains verifiable parental or lawful-guardian consent before a Child’s Personal Data is processed, unless School has documented a specific legal exemption applicable to that precise processing;
6. it obtains the adult student’s valid consent where consent is the applicable ground;
7. consent is free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous, evidenced by clear affirmative action, limited to necessary data, and capable of withdrawal with comparable ease;
8. it records the notice version, purposes, date and method of consent, identity of the consenting person, basis for confirming that person is an identifiable adult and parent or lawful guardian, and any withdrawal;
9. it does not send Provider a parent’s identity document or government identifier unless specifically required and agreed in writing;
10. it supplies only the minimum Personal Data needed for the selected Services;
11. it does not instruct Provider to use an identifiable student result for a public leaderboard, publication, direct marketing, or unrelated decision without the required separate permission;
12. it provides an appropriate human review process before using a GYS result for a material decision affecting a student; and
13. its authorised users maintain account confidentiality and promptly revoke access that is no longer required.

School will supply Provider with an age-status flag for each participating student:

- `UNDER_18`;
- `AGE_18_OR_OVER`; or
- `NOT_CONFIRMED`.

Provider will treat `NOT_CONFIRMED` as `UNDER_18` for consent and activation controls.

Before Provider activates School-led student processing, an authorised School administrator must attest:

> I confirm that the School has provided the applicable GYS data notice and has obtained and recorded all consent required for the students and selected modules, including verifiable parental or lawful-guardian consent for every participating student under 18. The School will notify GYS promptly of any withdrawal or relevant change.

Provider may request reasonable evidence of School’s consent process and records, but should not request copies of parent identity documents unless necessary for a specific compliance review and protected by additional controls.

## 6. Security

Provider will maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures proportionate to the nature of School Personal Data and the risk to students. At a minimum, the programme will include:

- encryption of Personal Data in transit and at rest;
- role-based, least-privilege access controls;
- multi-factor authentication for privileged and School-administrator access;
- logical separation of each school’s data;
- secure credential storage and account-recovery controls;
- access, processing, administrative, and security-event logs;
- monitoring, investigation, containment, remediation, and recurrence-prevention procedures;
- vulnerability management, security testing, patching, and secure development/change control;
- malware protection and network/service hardening;
- resilient backups, restoration testing, and business-continuity measures;
- vendor due diligence and written Subprocessor security obligations;
- secure deletion and media disposal;
- annual review of the documented information-security programme; and
- retention of required security and processing logs for the legally required period.

Provider will not materially reduce these safeguards during the service term.

## 9. Data accuracy and assessment decisions

Provider will provide reasonable tools and assistance for School to correct inaccurate roster or profile data.

Where Provider’s processing produces a score, tier, recommendation, integrity flag, or other output that may affect a student, Provider will:

- maintain reasonable quality, validation, and consistency controls;
- identify material known limitations in the relevant documentation;
- provide School an appropriate method to raise a suspected error; and
- not make a final disciplinary, admission, placement, or other high-impact decision for School.

School is responsible for appropriate human review and for considering educational context before making such a decision.

## 10. Data Principal requests and grievances

School is the primary point of contact for requests involving School Personal Data.

Provider will:

- forward a request received directly from a student, parent, guardian, or adult student to School without undue delay and no later than **five business days**, unless legally prohibited;
- not respond substantively except on School’s instruction or as required by law;
- provide reasonable search, access, correction, export, restriction, and deletion assistance;
- support School’s grievance investigation; and
- provide the information reasonably necessary for School to meet its legal response period.

If Provider is the Data Fiduciary for the data involved, Provider will respond through its own published rights and grievance process.

## 11. Personal Data Breach

Provider will notify School **without undue delay and, where feasible, within 24 hours** after becoming aware of a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting School Personal Data.

The initial notice will include information then reasonably available concerning:

- the nature, timing, location, and extent of the breach;
- affected systems, data categories, and approximate number of affected persons and records;
- likely consequences and risks to students;
- containment, mitigation, remediation, and recurrence-prevention measures;
- recommended protective steps for affected persons;
- the incident contact; and
- information needed for School’s notification to affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board of India.

Provider may provide information in phases and will supply material updates without undue delay. Provider will preserve relevant evidence, maintain an incident record, cooperate with School’s response, and not notify affected Data Principals or the Board on School’s behalf unless School instructs it or law requires Provider to do so.

The parties will coordinate public statements and notices where legally permitted.

## 12. Return, deletion, and retention

During the service term, School may export School Personal Data using available features or by a reasonable request.

Where School has selected a longitudinal profile, the retention periods in Schedule 2 may continue across individual assessment periods only while these Data Terms or a documented longitudinal archive arrangement remain in force and School continues to instruct Provider to maintain the profile. Expiry of an individual assessment purchase does not by itself end an active longitudinal archive arrangement.

After expiry or termination of the entire School relationship, including any longitudinal archive arrangement:

1. Provider will make an export available for **30 days**;
2. Provider will delete School Personal Data from active systems within **60 days** after the export period ends, unless School instructs an earlier lawful deletion;
3. residual backup copies will be overwritten or deleted within **90 additional days**; and
4. Provider may retain only data required by applicable law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

The Schedule 2 periods are maximum periods and do not authorise retention after the applicable purpose, instruction, or valid Consent has ended. Where lawful, Provider will delete data earlier upon School’s instruction, a valid withdrawal or erasure request communicated by School, or expiry of the applicable purpose. Legally retained data will be isolated from ordinary use, protected under these Data Terms, used only for the retention purpose, and deleted when that purpose ends.

The specific production retention periods are listed in Schedule 2 and the public Privacy Notice. The parties will verify technical implementation and obtain counsel approval before launch.

## 13. Compliance information and audit

On reasonable request, Provider will supply information needed to demonstrate compliance, such as a current security summary, Subprocessor list, relevant independent assessment or certification, and responses to a reasonable security questionnaire.

No more than once annually, or following a material Personal Data Breach or regulator request, School may request an additional audit of the controls relevant to School Personal Data. The audit must:

- use an independent qualified auditor;
- protect other customers’ information and Provider confidential information;
- avoid unreasonably disrupting operations;
- begin with existing reports and remote evidence where sufficient; and
- be at School’s cost unless it identifies a material breach by Provider.

Provider will promptly address a substantiated material finding.

## 14. Regulatory cooperation

Each party will reasonably assist the other with a binding enquiry, investigation, or order relating to processing under these Data Terms. Provider will notify School of a request for School Personal Data unless prohibited by law and will disclose only what the law requires.

## 15. Order of precedence and liability

If these Data Terms conflict with another part of the Agreement concerning the processing of School Personal Data, these Data Terms control unless a separately negotiated written agreement identifies the specific provision of these Data Terms to be overridden and expressly states the parties’ agreement to override it.

The liability provisions in the School Terms of Service and any applicable separately negotiated order apply to these Data Terms, subject to non-excludable liability under applicable law.

## 16. Changes and versions

Provider may update these Data Terms prospectively. Provider will notify the School before a material change affecting a current School order takes effect. Except where a change is required by law or reasonably necessary to address a material security risk, the version accepted by the School will continue to govern the current paid term, and a materially updated version will apply upon the School’s next renewal, new order, or express acceptance.

Provider will retain an accessible copy of each version and the effective date associated with the School’s electronic acceptance record.
